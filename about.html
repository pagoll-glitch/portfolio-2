The Student Journey

Honest Beginnings • Pune, India

I don't believe in pretending to have a decade of senior engineering credentials when I am just stepping onto this incredible journey. I am a first-year undergraduate student, and right now, my superpower is curiosity: an insatiable hunger to understand how machines think, how algorithms solve complex problems, and how thoughtful design can turn software into something that feels delightful to touch.

My journey began with simple Python scripts—watching logic statements execute in sequence, troubleshooting syntax errors, and experiencing that electrifying "it works!" moment. Since then, I've spent weekends sketching wireframes, learning semantic HTML and modern CSS layouts, collaborating with my peers at Newton School of Technology on hackathon solutions like UrbanPulse AI, and building prototypes like PathFinder.

This website itself is an expression of who I am: someone who refuses to separate technical rigor from poetic beauty. Inspired by vintage Parisian print magazines, café notebooks, and Pinterest typography, it reminds me every day that code can be as expressive and human as literature.
